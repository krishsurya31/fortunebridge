What We Offer

Seven ways to put your money to work

Every product on this page is one Fortune Bridge distributes directly. Nothing here is outsourced or notional.

Investments, from the most accessible to the most exclusive

Mutual Funds

Equity, debt and hybrid schemes for every goal and horizon. Start a monthly SIP or invest a lump sum.

SIF

Specialised Investment Funds, the new SEBI category between mutual funds and PMS, with focused and long-short strategies.

PMS

Portfolio Management Services: a professionally run portfolio of direct stocks, held in your own name.

AIF

Alternative Investment Funds across private equity, venture, credit and long-short strategies.

Stability, protection and liquidity

Bonds

Government securities, corporate bonds and NCDs for steady, predictable income alongside your equity holdings.

Insurance

Term, health and life cover, so a single event never derails the plan. Protection first, then growth.

LAMF

Loan Against Mutual Funds: a credit line by pledging your units, so you meet short-term needs without redeeming and without breaking compounding.
